# ColorShift — Story & World Lore

* **Studio:** Atomic Lemon Studio
* **Event:** GitHub Game Off 2026 (Theme: *Shift*)
* **Genre:** 2D Color-Shifting Puzzle-Platformer

---

## 1. Premise

In **ChromaVale**, an ancient, tranquil realm built among stone gardens, forgotten workshops, and prismatic ruins, color was never merely decorative—it gave weight, momentum, calm, and form to every corner of the world.

Everything changes when **Béchamel the Sixth**, a cunning and vain chameleon obsessed with hoarding beauty, absorbs every hue in the kingdom to keep it all for himself. Stripped of its spectrum, ChromaVale falls into a silent **grayscale**: objects remain in place, distinguishable only by the brightness or darkness of their neutral shade, yet dormant and inaccessible.

The kingdom's only hope rests on **Larry**, a steadfast bunny knight who discovers the **Chromoscope**—an ancient optical visor fitted with two rotating lenses capable of tinting perception and tuning into the latent colors hidden within the world.

---

## 2. Main Characters

### Larry (Protagonist)
* **Personality:** Observant, calm, determined, and resourceful. Larry doesn't seek fame or grand speeches; he acts simply because ChromaVale deserves its light back.
* **Motivation:** Track Béchamel the Sixth through the kingdom's chambers, master the Chromoscope, and restore every stolen color to its rightful place.
* **Connection to Gameplay:** Larry doesn't create color out of thin air; he shifts his perspective through the visor's lenses to interact with what the grayscale world conceals.

### Béchamel the Sixth (Antagonist / Final Boss)
* **Personality:** Theatrical, pompous, mocking, and elusive. As self-proclaimed royalty of camouflage, he delights in feeling untouchable because he can shift his appearance and vanish in plain sight.
* **Motivation:** Hoard the entire color spectrum as his personal royal collection and dictate what is seen and what stays hidden across ChromaVale.
* **Connection to Gameplay:** He embodies *shifting* driven by vanity and deception. At first, he treats Larry as a harmless nuisance, growing increasingly rattled as Larry learns to combine lenses.

## 3. The Chromoscope & World Logic

* **The Grayscale World (No Filter):** With no active lens, everything appears in grayscale based on its natural luminance (yellow is the brightest gray; blue is the darkest).
* **The Right Wheel (Levels 1–3):** Tunes into the three primary colors (**Red**, **Yellow**, and **Blue**). Looking through a color tints the environment with that hue, turns matching objects transparent and passable, reveals hidden elements of that color, and layers a matching emotional chord track onto the music.
* **The Left Wheel (Level 4 Onward):** Once recovered, it overlaps the right wheel, allowing Larry to **mix** primaries into secondary colors (**Orange**, **Green**, and **Violet**), unlocking paths and mechanisms a single hue cannot reach.
* **Camouflaged Enemies:** The insects patrolling ChromaVale share the chromatic nature of their surroundings. When Larry activates a filter matching an insect's color, it blends visually into the background and disappears from sight—while remaining a very real physical hazard.

---

## 4. Narrative Arc

The story unfolds across **three key narrative beats** designed to preserve brisk pacing for short play sessions:

1. **Act I — The Color Heist & The First Wheel (Intro / Level 1):** Béchamel the Sixth drains ChromaVale into grayscale and flees. Larry picks up the Chromoscope with its right wheel active and sets off in pursuit through the opening stages (Levels 1–3).
2. **Act II — The Second Wheel & Color Mixing (Level 4):** After overcoming the initial trials, Larry discovers the left wheel. Overlapping both lenses unlocks secondary color combinations, disrupting Béchamel's plans across the mid-to-late stages (Levels 4–8, plus optional Levels 9–10).
3. **Act III — The Final Confrontation & Return of Color (Final Level / Credits):** Larry corners Béchamel the Sixth in the final chamber. Following a heated exchange and the final trial, the stolen spectrum breaks free from Béchamel's grasp, restoring color to ChromaVale before rolling the credits.
