**Atomic Lemon Studio** is a six-person team making **ColorShift** for GitHub Game Off 2026 (theme: *Shift*).

## The game

<!-- TODO: game icon / key art. Suggested path: /assets/img/colorshift-icon.png -->
<div class="image-placeholder">
  Game icon
</div>

Someone has stolen the world's color. A bunny knight explores it with the **Chromoscope**, a viewer with two colored discs that tints whatever she looks at and reveals what the theft left hidden. Mix the discs, shift the color and solve the puzzles.

- **Genre:** 2D puzzle platformer
- **Platform:** web (itch.io), desktop and mobile

## Design pillars

1. **Puzzles:** short levels with a single solution each.
2. **Colors:** three primaries and three mixes, from two discs.
3. **Secrets:** hidden objects that only appear under the right filter.
